**Spain Midfield Picture:** On paper the best midfield in the world — on practice, the biggest anxiety. Without him at full fitness, Spain's game management changes entirely. Pedri and Dani Olmo provide the creativity; Fabián Ruiz provides a reliable backup option. Zubimendi (not on this list) would fill in if Rodri misses games. Spain's ceiling with a fit Rodri is the tournament's highest; their floor without him is much more mortal.

## 🚫 Countries That Did NOT Qualify

### Italy — Three Consecutive Misses

| Player | Club | Country | Reason |
|--------|------|---------|--------|
| Manuel Locatelli | Juventus | Italy | Lost WC playoff final to Bosnia on penalties |
| Sandro Tonali | Newcastle | Italy | Lost WC playoff final to Bosnia on penalties |
| Nicolò Barella | Inter Milan | Italy | Lost WC playoff final to Bosnia on penalties |

Italy's third consecutive World Cup absence is the tournament's single greatest structural absence. This generation of midfielders — Barella, Tonali, Locatelli — may be Italy's deepest in 20 years. Barella (3G/8A in Serie A, Scudetto winner) is arguably the best midfielder NOT at the World Cup. Tonali has the added cruelty of having missed 2022 on a betting ban. It's unprecedented for a football nation of Italy's stature to miss three consecutive World Cups, and this time the quality of the squad makes the absence harder to explain.

### Denmark — Playoff Final Loss to Czechia

| Player | Club | Country | Reason |
|--------|------|---------|--------|
| Pierre-Emile Højbjerg | Marseille | Denmark | Lost UEFA playoff final to Czechia |

Denmark's loss to Czechia in the playoff final sends their experienced captain home. Højbjerg (31 by the tournament) had the quality to contribute and the leadership to influence a squad. At his age, this was almost certainly his final realistic World Cup opportunity.
