### Italy: The Complete Defensive Picture Lost
Italy's absence removes what may have been the tournament's best defensive trio: Bastoni, Di Lorenzo, and Romagnoli — three Serie A regulars who collectively have dominated their league all season. Bastoni alone (4 assists from CB, Scudetto winner, arguably a top-3 CB in the world) would have transformed any national defence. Three consecutive WC absences from a nation of Italy's defensive tradition is without precedent in the modern era.
