*Data as of May 10, 2026 | Source: FotMob, LiveScore, Transfermarkt, ESPN, Bundesliga.com, UEFA.com*

The 2026 FIFA World Cup kicks off June 11, 2026 across USA, Canada & Mexico. Squad announcements begin late May.

---

## GK Metrics Framework

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Save %** | Percentage of shots on target saved — basic workload quality |
| **PSxG-GA** | Post-shot xG minus goals allowed — positive = saving above expectation (best GK quality measure) |
| **Clean Sheets** | Zero-goals-conceded matches — context-dependent on team defence |
| **GK Style** | Sweeper-keeper / Shot-stopper / Distribution-elite / Ball-playing |

## Key Storylines

### Italy's Triple GK Tragedy
No WC position better illustrates Italy's qualification catastrophe than goalkeeper. **Donnarumma** (Manchester City, 26) is arguably the world's second-best goalkeeper right now — 6ft5, elite reflexes, improving with his feet, never once appeared at a World Cup. **Di Gregorio** (Juventus, 26) has posted one of Serie A's best PSxG-GA figures and earned a starting role at a Champions League club in his debut season there. **Vicario** (Tottenham, 28) has been hampered by Spurs' defensive chaos but his 6 UCL clean sheets in 10 apps prove the quality behind the league numbers. Three Europe-level GKs watching from home because their country lost a playoff final on penalties for the third consecutive time.

### Oblak: The World Cup That Never Came
**Jan Oblak** is 33 years old with 112 Atlético Madrid appearances and arguably the most consistent defensive record of any goalkeeper in La Liga history. He has also never played a World Cup match. Slovenia lost all their qualifying matches and finished bottom of their group. Despite being routinely ranked top-5 in the world by UEFA and FIFA technical studies, Oblak's international resume will have zero World Cup appearances. He joins Donnarumma and Di Gregorio in a group of genuinely elite goalkeepers for whom the 2026 WC simply does not exist.

### Neuer's Final Chapter
**Manuel Neuer** confirmed in February 2026 that he will not return to the Germany squad for the 2026 World Cup. The retirement was not forced — Germany qualified with relative comfort — but Neuer's own assessment that he could not be the starting goalkeeper at 40 led to the decision. The man who invented the modern sweeper-keeper role, who won the 2018 Golden Glove, will watch Germany from the outside as **Oliver Baumann** and **Alexander Nübel** battle for the No.1 jersey. A ceremonially appropriate ending — announcing it himself, on his own terms — but the timing (1 month before squad selection) left Germany's GK situation with unexpected urgency.

### The Dual Fitness Concern: Courtois and Alisson
The two most impactful goalkeepers at this tournament beyond Maignan are both racing against injury:
- **Thibaut Courtois** (Belgium) — quad/muscle injury March 2026; targeting return around May 10. IFFHS named him World's Best GK for 2025-26 before the injury. When fit, he is simply the best in the world. Belgium with Courtois at 80% fitness and with Courtois ruled out are two entirely different tournament propositions.
- **Alisson Becker** (Liverpool) — hamstring since March 18; has not played since; described as "very close" to return. Brazil confirmed their March squad without him. With 45 international clean sheets in 75 caps he remains the tournament's most decorated active GK statistically. The question is whether a goalkeeper returning from months out can hit peak form by June 11.

### England's Crowded Third GK Race
Pickford is England's unchallenged No.1 (96%). The No.2 spot is likely going to whichever club GK is in best form come squad announcement. The genuine contest is for third:
- **Trafford** (65%) — 1 senior cap, U21 Euros winner, backed by Tuchel's coaching staff, 8 ESPN panel members name him
- **Henderson** (55%) — PSxG-GA +5.0 (2nd in PL), Palace captain, statistically the most qualified — but zero senior caps in this window
- **Pope** (30%) — 40+ caps, experience, but concussion in November and a stop-start season at 34 diminishes his case

The statistical case says Henderson; the coaching preference says Trafford. A call that will define Tuchel's first tournament squad selection.

### Joan García: The La Liga Best Nobody Knows
**Joan García** joined Barcelona from Espanyol in summer 2025 and has proceeded to post La Liga's best PSxG-GA figure (+7.68) while keeping 12 clean sheets in 28 appearances. He has been confirmed in Spain's WC squad (May 9 reports). He also has exactly 1 senior international cap. Spain's GK depth is now the deepest in world football: a starting goalkeeper (Simón) who outperforms internationally vs. domestically, a backup (Raya) with three PL Golden Gloves, and a third-choice (García) who is La Liga's objective best GK this season. Remarkable.

### The Grimaldo Paradox of Goalkeeping: Raya vs Simón
David Raya's PSxG-GA (~+7 in PL) rivals Joan García for Europe's best GK metric this season. He is arguably performing better week-to-week than Unai Simón. Yet Simón remains Spain's unquestioned No.1 because De la Fuente trusts his distribution system (90.1% pass accuracy in qualifiers) and his role initiating Spain's build-up. Raya's 18 clean sheets and 36 league appearances make the case — but Simón starts, and Raya provides the world's most effective backup option.
