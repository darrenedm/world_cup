## Key Storylines

### The Unlucky Ones (Great Form, No World Cup)
The biggest tragedy of the list: **Guirassy** (16 Bundesliga goals), **Kvaratskhelia** (7G/4A at PSG), **Højlund** (9 goals on loan at Napoli), and **Mbeumo** (9 PL goals for Man Utd) are all in excellent form but their countries will not be in North America this summer. Guirassy's Guinea and Mbeumo's Cameroon both fell in African qualifying; Kvaratskhelia's Georgia couldn't navigate UEFA playoffs; Højlund's Denmark lost to Czechia in the final playoff round.

### The Injury Wild Card
**Isak** and **Lukaku** are the two players most likely to drop off this list before squads are named. Isak managed only 3 goals in 16 appearances for Liverpool after his British-record £125m move, while Lukaku's high-grade rectus femoris injury will keep him sidelined for at least five months. Both are still expected to travel if fit — Isak would be Sweden's best striker alongside Gyökeres, and Lukaku is Belgium's all-time top scorer.

### The Form Story
**Luis Díaz** at Bayern Munich is the season's breakout star: 15 goals and 13 assists in the Bundesliga, the first Bayern player to reach 13G/13A in a single season since records began. **Doué** at PSG has posted 26 goals across five competitions — a stunning emergence. **Kane** is on course for a third successive Bundesliga Golden Boot.

### The Greenwood Question
Greenwood is the most extreme club vs. country mismatch on this list: 18 goals and 7 assists across all competitions for Marseille, yet a 5% probability for England. The FA have not called him up since his legal case and the political/reputational bar remains very high despite his form.
