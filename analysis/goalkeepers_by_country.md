**The quality of absent goalkeepers:** Donnarumma at 26 is arguably the world's second-best GK by conventional metrics — his combination of size, reflexes, and improving footwork put him at the absolute elite level. The Oblak case is arguably the most poignant in this position group — a top-5 all-time GK with zero World Cup appearances.

### Italy: The Complete GK Picture Lost
Three elite Italian GKs, three separate clubs (Manchester City, Juventus, Tottenham), three entirely different styles. Donnarumma (traditional shot-stopper, elite reflexes), Di Gregorio (modern ball-playing keeper, positive PSxG-GA at Juve), Vicario (distribution-focused, 6 UCL CS revealing quality). Any of the three would strengthen a qualifying nation's squad. None of them will be in the tournament.

### Neuer: End of an Era
Germany qualified but their greatest goalkeeper retired. Neuer's international career ends with 124 caps, the 2014 World Cup, the 2018 Golden Glove, and the legacy of having invented modern sweeper-keeping as a widespread tactical template. Germany now resolves their GK position with a squad announcement that will likely favour Baumann or Nübel — neither carrying anywhere near Neuer's credentials. The gap is not just in talent but in experience under tournament pressure.
